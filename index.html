Everything you need to know about our services, working methodology, timelines, and technology stack.

Virexo Innovations is a comprehensive digital agency offering 6 core disciplines:

  • Web Development: High-performance React, Next.js, and full-stack web applications.
  • UI/UX Design: Interactive wireframing, clickable prototypes, and Figma design systems.
  • AI & Automation: Bespoke conversational chatbots, n8n/Zapier autonomous workflows, and LLM integrations.
  • Python Development: Enterprise FastAPI/Django microservices, resilient web scrapers, and data pipelines.
  • Digital Marketing: High-intent Google/Meta paid advertising, technical SEO, and conversion rate optimization (CRO).
  • Social Media Growth: Viral reels, short-form video editing, and organic multi-platform scaling.

Our web applications use high-performance, GPU-friendly IntersectionObserver triggers combined with cubic easing curves. As soon as a stat element scrolls into view, the numbers calculate dynamically from zero up to the final target value in real time at smooth 60 frames per second without causing any layout shifts or battery drain.

Timelines vary based on scope and requirements:

  • Landing Pages & UI/UX Redesigns: 5 to 10 business days.
  • Custom Web Applications & E-commerce: 2 to 4 weeks.
  • Custom AI Workflow Automation & Python Scraping: 1 to 3 weeks.
  • Social Media Growth Retainers: Ongoing monthly sprint cycles.

We work in agile sprint cycles and provide functional staging links at each milestone for your review.

We conduct a discovery audit of your existing manual processes, customer support volume, and software tools.
